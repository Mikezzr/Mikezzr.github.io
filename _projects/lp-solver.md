I studied the Forrest-Tomlin update used by simplex-based linear programming solvers and identified special permutation properties in the underlying matrix structures.

The resulting optimization achieved a **10%-20% speedup** on benchmark linear programming problems. This work was conducted at the Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data with Prof. Xiaodong Luo.
